## Problem

Flat maps struggle to show the scale and directionality of forced migration across centuries. Viewers need spatial context for regional powers and cultural identities — not only trade-route overlays.

## Approach

An interactive 3D globe covering **1400–1900 CE**, with a full user guide in-repo (`docs/GUIDE.md`). Local preview is intentional and simple:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

No framework tax — JavaScript, CSS, and HTML aimed at clarity of motion and readable panels over spectacle.

## Outcome

A self-hosted atlas for education and research demos, pairing geographic exploration with regional and cultural framing of the trade’s impact.
