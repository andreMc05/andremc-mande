## Problem

Independent artists juggle splits spreadsheets, ISRC ownership, stems readiness, and long-horizon rights calendars across too many tools. Music commerce belongs on a dedicated music site; **catalog hygiene** still needs a focused workspace.

## Approach

Vanilla ES modules (no build step for the runtime app) with CSS custom properties and `localStorage` persistence. Core surfaces:

- **Splits tracker** — roles, visual split bars, auto-balance to 100%
- **Asset health** — Full Mix / Instrumental / Stems / Clean / ID3 checklist with Sync Ready badge
- **Life cycle timeline** — Escape Velocity → Sync/Stagnation → Retroactive Renaissance, plus a 35-year termination clock
- **ISRC registry** and a **Promote & Stream** platform directory
- **Registration guide** for LLC and rights roadmap phases

## Outcome

A local-first music catalog cockpit aimed at metadata sovereignty and sync readiness — complementary to (not competing with) the separate music sales site.
