## Problem

Teams confuse automated scans with Section 508 conformance. Products that print “508 compliant” or invent a compliance score create legal risk. I needed a **pre-audit rater** that is explicit about owners, packs, and limits.

## Approach

Working title was Quick508 Rater; the shipped name is **508 Draft Rater** so it cannot be mistaken for a conformance engine. Scope for v1:

- Revised Section 508 electronic content: **E205.4 → WCAG 2.0 Level A and AA** (38 SCs)
- Page-level draft status only: does not meet / incomplete / no failures on tested criteria
- Optional 0–100 **risk/coverage index** — never a compliance score
- Owners: `code`, `axe`, `jev` (semantic judgments), and `human` for runtime/site-set SCs

Jev is not the decision maker. Deterministic fails from code + axe cannot be overruled by vibes.

## Outcome

A tooling stance that matches how federal accessibility work actually signs off — humans on ACR/VPAT, machines on inventory and mapped WCAG signals.
